Topic: Six Principles of Effective Management
Date: 14th August 2026

1) Identify goals and Constraints: Managers must clearly define the objectives they aim to achieve and understand the limitations they face, such as budget constraints, resource availability, and regulatory requirements.

2) Recognize the nature and importance of profits: Managers should understand that profits are essential for the sustainability and growth of a business. They must focus on strategies that maximize profits while balancing ethical considerations and long-term goals.

3) Understand incentives: Managers need to comprehend the motivations and incentives of both producers and consumers. This understanding helps in designing effective strategies that align with the interests of all stakeholders.

4) Undestand Markets: Managers should have a deep understanding of different market structures, such as competitive markets, monopolies, and oligopolies. This knowledge allows them to make informed decisions regarding pricing, production, and market entry strategies.

5) Recognize the time value of money: Managers must appreciate that the value of money changes over time due to factors like inflation and interest rates. This principle is crucial for making investment decisions, evaluating projects, and managing cash flows effectively.

6) Use marginal analysis

Principle: Cost, Properly Defined:
- Opportunity Cost: The value of the next best alternative you give up to make your primary choice. In cash, effort, or time - whether or not money changes hands.
- Explicit Cost: The actual cash out-lay.
- Implicit Cost: No cash leaves hand, but something is given up - like time, effort, or opportunity.
This matters more than accounting cost since accounting cost only considers money actually spent, while economic cost includes both explicit and implicit costs. Managers must consider both types of costs to make informed decisions that maximize overall value and efficiency.
